Future Technology Scholarship

Offered by FutureTech Foundation

Scholarship Amount

$5,000

Deadline

June 30, 2026

Location

Worldwide

About the Scholarship

The Future Technology Scholarship supports students who are passionate about technology and want to build a career in the technology industry.

Eligibility

  • Must be currently enrolled in a university.
  • Must have a minimum GPA of 3.0.
  • Must be studying technology or a related field.
  • Must submit an application before the deadline.

Application Requirements

  • Academic transcript
  • Personal statement
  • Proof of enrollment
  • Letter of recommendation
